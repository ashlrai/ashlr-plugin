---
title: Confidence badges
description: The "ashlr confidence" footer on compressed tool output — when it appears, how the tier is computed, and how to recover full fidelity.
---

When an ashlr tool shortens its output, it appends a one-line confidence footer that says how much of the original survived:

```
[ashlr confidence: medium · 3KB→1KB · bypassSummary:true recovers fidelity]
```

The footer names the tier, the raw and returned sizes, and how to get the full output back. It comes from `confidenceBadge` in `servers/_summarize.ts`, which the tools that compress output share, including `ashlr__read`, `ashlr__grep`, `ashlr__bash`, `ashlr__diff`, `ashlr__logs`, `ashlr__sql`, `ashlr__http` and `ashlr__webfetch`.

## When it appears

- When the output was shortened (fewer bytes returned than the raw output had) and the raw output was at least 512 bytes. Small or verbatim results carry no footer.
- Always when the LLM summarizer failed and the tool fell back to truncation, or when a command exited non-zero and output was elided. Both are reported as `low`, whatever the size.

## Tiers

The tier is the ratio of returned bytes to raw bytes:

| Tier | Condition |
|---|---|
| `high` | Returned output is at least 1/3 of the raw size. |
| `medium` | Returned output is at least 1/8 of the raw size. |
| `low` | Less than 1/8 survived, nothing survived, the LLM summarizer fell back to truncation, or a command exited non-zero with output elided. |

The footer stays within 80 characters. When the full line would be longer, the byte sizes are dropped first.

## What to do with it

- `high`: most of the output is present.
- `medium`: a large part was elided or summarized. Before a targeted edit, check the exact lines.
- `low`: most of the output is gone. Re-run the call with `bypassSummary: true`, as the footer says, or narrow the request.

Compressed output is a navigation aid. Omitted content is not absent; retrieve it before editing or verifying behavior.
