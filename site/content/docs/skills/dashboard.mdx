## When to use

When you want more than [`/ashlr-savings`](/docs/skills/savings) shows: lifetime savings by tool, activity over the last 30 days, a projection, and which projects use ashlr most.

## What it does

Runs `bun run ${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/scripts/savings-dashboard.ts` and prints its output verbatim in a code block. The script reads `~/.ashlr/stats.json` and `~/.ashlr/session-log.jsonl` and renders, in order:

1. The ashlr banner and a today-vs-yesterday line (skipped when the numbers are quiet)
2. Session, lifetime and best-day tiles
3. Per-tool lifetime savings: bar, tokens, tokens per call and share
4. Where savings come from, and a 7-day adoption funnel (both read the session log)
5. Activity sparklines for the last 7 and 30 days
6. Projected annual savings: 30-day rolling × 12, active days × 220, and calls
7. Top 3 projects by call count, from the session log
8. Hook performance, upgrade nudge and streak panels, when there is data for them
9. A footer with the stats path, pricing model and saved/raw ratio

Colors are used only when the terminal reports truecolor and `NO_COLOR` is unset. Pass `--by-tool` to `/ashlr-dashboard` to also append the per-tool report from `scripts/session-log-report.ts` under a `## By tool` heading.

With no `stats.json` yet, it prints the banner and `no savings recorded yet`. The projection needs at least 3 active days in the last 30.
